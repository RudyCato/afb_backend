Hosting the AFB Public Facing Site



\- **Magento**

* Magento is a good choice if:

  * You expect high traffic and growth
  * You need custom features
  * You run a B2B business
  * You want full control over your store
  * 

**Magento may not be ideal if:**



  * You are a beginner
  * You have a low budget
  * You need a quick and simple setup
