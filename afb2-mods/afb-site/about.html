About Us

Established in 1989. Still based in New Jersey, USA.

American Food & Beverage was established in 1989 as an importer of food products, and evolved over the next 20 years — producing juices, vinegars, preserves, jams, honey and pickled products. We now own four manufacturing facilities on 10 acres of land, and remain committed to providing high quality products at competitive prices.

Our facilities

Four manufacturing facilities. Ten acres. New Jersey.

What started as a single import operation has grown into a vertically integrated manufacturing and distribution company. Receiving, cold and dry storage, roasting, flavoring, chocolate panning, blending, packing and shipping all happen across our New Jersey campus.

We're proud to still be based in New Jersey, USA — the same state where we started over three decades ago.

How we started

In 1989 we began as an importer of food products. Over the following two decades we expanded into production — juices, vinegars, preserves, jams, honey and pickled products — building the infrastructure and expertise to control quality at every step.

What we make today

Nuts & seeds, dried fruits, superfood powders, chocolate-covered items, snacks and confections — sourced direct from growers and processors worldwide, finished and packed at our own facilities in New Jersey.

Who we serve

Corporate supermarkets, independent grocery stores, distributors, restaurants, hotels, airlines and the broader food service industry. Retail customers can order direct through this site.

Our commitment

Still committed to providing high quality products. SQF Certified, USDA Organic, and OU Kosher — because the certifications our customers need are non-negotiable.

Our brands

Three brands, one standard.

We run three brands out of our New Jersey facilities: Grassland for organic and natural, Premium Food for the gourmet line, and Tasty for the everyday screw-top jar range. Same floor, same quality standards across all three.
