    Our Story

    Feeding America since 1989

    What started as a small importer in Paterson, NJ has grown over 35 years into one of the East Coast's leading bulk-food processors — still family-operated, still in Paterson.

    Four facilities on 10 acres

    All roasting, blending, flavoring, packing, and shipping happens on our own NJ campus. No co-packers, no surprises.

    Direct-source buying

    Our buyers travel annually to origin — California, Chile, Turkey, Iran, India, Vietnam — securing crop position direct from growers.

    SQF Level 2 certified

    Safe Quality Food Level 2 certification covers our full manufacturing process. Third-party audited annually.

    USDA Organic & OU Kosher

    Organic certification for qualifying products; OU Kosher across the full line. Certificates available on request.
